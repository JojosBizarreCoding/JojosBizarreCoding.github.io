My name is Alice Janssen. I'm a student at Graphic Lyceum Rotterdam. I'm in my second year of the Software Development course. I like to play guitar, play video games and make pixel art. I have 2 years of experience with HTML, JavaScript and CSS. I have 1.5 years of experience with php, phpMyAdmin and database development in general. I have experience with unity and I have a unity certificate. I also recently started developing in Godot.
